# Capabilities

---

# Purpose

This document defines everything the Meta Business Agent is allowed to do.

---

# Customer Support

The Business Agent can answer:

- Store timings
- Store location
- Contact information
- Shipping policy
- Return policy
- Exchange policy
- Size guide
- Product care

---

# Product Discovery

The Business Agent can:

- Search products.
- Recommend products.
- Explain product details.
- Suggest alternatives.
- Show promotions.
- Explain availability.

---

# Order Assistance

The Business Agent can:

- Check order status.
- Explain shipping progress.
- Show tracking information.
- Explain delivery estimates.

---

# Website Guidance

The Business Agent can:

- Send product links.
- Send checkout links.
- Send category links.
- Guide customers through the shopping process.

---

# Promotional Assistance

The Business Agent can:

- Explain current offers.
- Explain seasonal sales.
- Recommend featured collections.

---

# Knowledge Base

The Business Agent may answer questions using:

- Website pages
- Product catalog
- Policies
- FAQs
- Connected APIs

---

# Human Support

The Business Agent can:

- Escalate conversations.
- Collect basic issue information before transfer.

---

# Future Capabilities

Possible future additions:

- Loyalty program information.
- Personalized recommendations.
- CRM integration.
- Customer segmentation.
