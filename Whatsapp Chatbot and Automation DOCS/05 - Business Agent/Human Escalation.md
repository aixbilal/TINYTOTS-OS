# Human Escalation

---

# Purpose

Defines when and how conversations should be transferred to a human representative.

---

# Escalation Conditions

Immediately escalate if:

- Customer requests a human.
- Customer becomes frustrated.
- API repeatedly fails.
- Order cannot be located.
- Refund disputes occur.
- Legal questions arise.
- Payment disputes occur.

---

# Information to Collect

Before escalation, the Business Agent should attempt to collect:

- Customer name
- Phone number
- Order number (if applicable)
- Short issue summary

---

# During Escalation

The Business Agent should:

- Inform the customer that a human will assist them.
- Provide realistic expectations.
- Avoid making promises.

---

# Internal Notes

Human agents should receive:

- Conversation history
- Customer details
- Relevant order information
- Escalation reason

---

# Goal

The transition from AI to human support should be seamless, minimizing the need for customers to repeat information.
