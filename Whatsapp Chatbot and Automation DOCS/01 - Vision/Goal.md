# Goals

---

# Primary Goals

The WhatsApp platform exists to solve specific business problems.

Its goals are divided into Business Goals, Technical Goals, and Customer Experience Goals.

---

# Business Goals

- Reduce customer support workload.
- Improve customer communication.
- Increase customer engagement.
- Improve order transparency.
- Deliver promotions automatically.
- Increase customer retention.
- Improve operational efficiency.

---

# Technical Goals

Build a system that is:

- Modular
- Scalable
- Secure
- Easy to maintain
- Easy to extend
- Reliable
- Event-driven

The system should avoid unnecessary complexity and duplicated business logic.

---

# Customer Experience Goals

Customers should be able to:

- Receive instant answers.
- Check order status.
- Discover products.
- Find store information.
- Receive automatic notifications.
- Contact support easily.

The overall experience should require minimal effort from the customer.

---

# Development Goals

The implementation should:

- Minimize custom chatbot code.
- Leverage Meta Business Agent capabilities.
- Use n8n for workflow automation.
- Keep APIs lightweight.
- Keep Supabase as the single source of truth.

---

# Performance Goals

The platform should:

- Respond quickly.
- Scale as customer volume increases.
- Handle failures gracefully.
- Support future integrations.

---

# Maintenance Goals

The architecture should allow developers to:

- Add new workflows without major code changes.
- Update prompts independently.
- Extend APIs without rewriting existing systems.
- Replace automation tools if needed.

---

# Long-Term Goals

Support future features including:

- Loyalty program
- CRM integration
- Marketing campaigns
- AI personalization
- Analytics
- Customer insights
