# TinyTots User Frontend — Our Story

## Purpose

Our Story communicates the brand worldview and establishes emotional differentiation.

## Direction

This page should feel closer to a fashion editorial than a corporate About page.

## Structure

Possible:

```text
HERO
↓
BRAND STATEMENT
↓
ORIGIN / STORY
↓
VALUES
↓
CRAFT / QUALITY
↓
IMAGERY
↓
CURRENT BRAND WORLD
↓
SHOP CTA
```

Final content must come from approved brand material.

## Visual Language

Prioritize:

- photography
- typography
- whitespace
- editorial composition
- restrained motion

## Motion

Potential:

- cinematic image reveals
- typography transitions
- controlled scroll choreography
- subtle depth

## 3D

Use only if it contributes to storytelling.

## Copy

Avoid generic luxury language without specific meaning.

The page should communicate actual brand beliefs and facts.

## Conversion

The page may conclude with a subtle path into:

- collections
- new arrivals
- featured products

## Definition of Done

The visitor should leave with a clearer understanding of TinyTots as a brand, not merely as a retailer.
