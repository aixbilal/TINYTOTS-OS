# TinyTots User Frontend — Shipping & Returns

## Purpose

Communicate delivery and return policies clearly before purchase.

## Structure

```text
SHIPPING
├── Coverage
├── Delivery expectations
├── Charges
└── Tracking

RETURNS
├── Eligibility
├── Process
├── Conditions
└── Refund timing
```

Actual content must come from approved policy.

## UX

Use clear sections and strong scanning hierarchy.

## FAQ

Frequently asked policy questions may be grouped into accordions.

## Trust

Do not hide important restrictions in fine print.

## Motion

Minimal.

## Cross Links

Useful links may include:

- Track Order
- Help Center
- Contact
- Size Guide

## Definition of Done

A customer can understand the policy without needing to interpret ambiguous language.
