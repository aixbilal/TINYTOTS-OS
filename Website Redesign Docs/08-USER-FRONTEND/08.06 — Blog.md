# TinyTots User Frontend — Blog

## Purpose

The Blog/Journal experience provides editorial content and supports brand discovery.

## Direction

Prefer a curated journal aesthetic over a generic CMS listing.

## Structure

```text
HEADER
↓
JOURNAL HERO
↓
FEATURED STORY
↓
ARTICLE GRID
↓
CATEGORIES / TAGS
↓
PAGINATION OR LOAD MORE
↓
FOOTER
```

## Article Card

Show:

- image
- title
- short metadata
- category where useful
- date where appropriate

## Featured Story

The featured story may receive:

- larger image
- stronger typography
- more generous spacing

## Article Page

The article should prioritize:

- title
- metadata
- hero image
- readable content
- related stories

## Commerce Integration

Product links may appear naturally where editorial context supports them.

Do not turn every article into an advertisement.

## Motion

Use image and typography reveals sparingly.

## Performance

Editorial images must be optimized.

## Definition of Done

The journal feels like part of the TinyTots brand world rather than an unrelated content subsystem.
