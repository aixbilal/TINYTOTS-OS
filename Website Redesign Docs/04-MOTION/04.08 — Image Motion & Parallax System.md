# TinyTots Image Motion & Parallax

## 1. Purpose

Image movement should make photography feel alive.

## 2. Techniques

Approved candidates:

- slow scale
- subtle translation
- reveal masks
- clip-path transitions
- crossfade
- depth-based parallax

## 3. Hero

Hero images may use:

- initial scale settling
- subtle scroll movement
- text/image choreography

## 4. Product

Product images should remain stable enough for shopping.

Avoid excessive movement that makes comparison difficult.

## 5. Editorial

Editorial images can have stronger motion.

## 6. Crop Safety

Motion must not reveal empty image edges.

## 7. Performance

Use:

- transform
- opacity
- compositor-friendly properties

Avoid unnecessary continuous repainting.

## 8. Reduced Motion

Use a static image or very short opacity transition.

## 9. Acceptance Criteria

Image motion must preserve product/image comprehension.
