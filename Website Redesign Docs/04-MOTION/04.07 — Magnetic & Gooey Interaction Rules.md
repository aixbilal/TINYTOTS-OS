# TinyTots Magnetic & Gooey Interaction Rules

## 1. Purpose

Magnetic and gooey effects are experimental brand-expression tools.

They are not default UI behavior.

## 2. Magnetic Buttons

Potential use:

- hero CTA
- campaign CTA
- selected premium desktop interactions

Do not apply magnetic behavior to every button.

## 3. Magnetic Parameters

A production implementation must document:

- attraction radius
- maximum displacement
- spring behavior
- pointer tracking
- touch fallback
- reduced-motion fallback

## 4. Touch

On touch devices:

- disable pointer-following
- preserve normal button behavior

## 5. Gooey

Gooey effects may be explored for:

- decorative campaign transitions
- abstract background shapes
- selected navigation experiments

## 6. Do Not Use Gooey Effects For

- forms
- product pricing
- cart totals
- important alerts
- data tables
- accessibility-critical controls

## 7. Visual Rule

Gooey effects should feel sophisticated and abstract, not cartoon-like.

## 8. Performance

SVG filters and blur-heavy effects can be expensive.

Test on:

- desktop
- mid-range laptop
- mobile

## 9. Acceptance Criteria

The site must remain premium and fully usable with all gooey/magnetic effects removed.
