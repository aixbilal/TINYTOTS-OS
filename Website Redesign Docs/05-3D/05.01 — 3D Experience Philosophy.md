# TinyTots 3D Experience Philosophy

## Purpose

3D is an optional premium experience layer, not the visual identity of the entire website.

The goal is to create moments that feel:

- cinematic
- tactile
- contemporary
- editorial
- premium
- intentional

without making the website feel like a technology demonstration.

## Core Principle

> Use 3D when depth materially improves the story, product understanding, or emotional impact.

Do not use 3D merely because it is technically possible.

## Brand Direction

The 3D language should support the TinyTots positioning:

- premium children's fashion
- calm confidence
- sophisticated restraint
- high-quality materials
- editorial photography
- modern luxury

It should avoid:

- childish game-like environments
- excessive neon
- generic futuristic scenes
- uncontrolled particle effects
- visual clutter
- gimmicky physics

## Experience Hierarchy

```text
BRAND
  ↓
CONTENT
  ↓
PRODUCT
  ↓
INTERACTION
  ↓
3D
```

3D never outranks the product or message.

## Appropriate Uses

Strong candidates:

- campaign hero
- seasonal launch
- product material exploration
- subtle 3D product presentation
- immersive editorial section
- controlled background depth

Weak candidates:

- support pages
- checkout
- account utility pages
- size guide
- shipping information

## Art Direction

Preferred characteristics:

- soft studio lighting
- controlled shadows
- restrained materials
- neutral or brand-aligned environments
- realistic scale
- deliberate camera movement
- high-quality composition

## Motion Philosophy

3D movement should feel physical rather than chaotic.

Use:

- slow camera movement
- subtle parallax
- gentle rotation
- controlled object transitions
- depth changes

Avoid constant movement.

## Premium Test

Before approving a 3D experience ask:

1. Does it improve the story?
2. Does it improve product perception?
3. Would the page be weaker without it?
4. Is the performance cost justified?
5. Does the experience still work without it?

If the answer to several is no, remove the 3D.

## Definition of Done

A 3D experience is complete only when:

- visual purpose is documented
- interaction is documented
- performance is measured
- mobile behavior exists
- reduced-motion behavior exists
- fallback exists
- loading behavior exists
