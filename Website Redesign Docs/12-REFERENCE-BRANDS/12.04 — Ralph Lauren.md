# TinyTots Reference Brand — Ralph Lauren

## Purpose

Lifestyle aspiration, world-building, consistent identity, premium storytelling. Borrow the principle, not unsupported heritage or cultural styling.

This is a **principle reference, not a template**.

## Core Lessons

### World-Building
Premium brands sell an emotional world around products.

### Lifestyle Photography
Products can live naturally inside a broader lifestyle narrative.

### Aspiration
Help customers imagine the child, occasion, family, and lifestyle associated with the product.

### Consistency
Repeated visual and editorial principles build recognition over time.

## TinyTots Application

Use the reference primarily for:

- visual hierarchy
- brand storytelling
- composition
- interaction decisions
- photography direction
- premium perception

Specific application must remain consistent with TinyTots' governing design system.

## What Not to Copy

Do not reproduce:

- exact layouts
- exact typography
- exact photography
- brand-specific color systems
- proprietary visual signatures
- patterns that conflict with TinyTots usability, accessibility, or performance
