# Touch Interaction

## Purpose

Define interaction behavior for touch-capable devices.

## Principle

Touch interfaces require intentional hit areas and feedback.

## Targets

Interactive controls must provide comfortable touch targets and adequate spacing.

Avoid placing multiple destructive or unrelated actions immediately adjacent.

## Gestures

Use gestures only when they are:

- discoverable
- reversible
- non-essential where accessibility requires alternatives

Do not make swipe the only way to access critical functionality.

## Product Media

Swipe galleries are appropriate where useful.

Provide visible controls or accessible alternatives where appropriate.

## Menus

Touch menus should:

- open predictably
- remain stable while interacting
- close through explicit mechanisms
- avoid accidental activation

## Buttons

Touch feedback should be immediate.

Do not use hover-only feedback on touch devices.

## Forms

Support:

- correct input types
- autofill
- appropriate keyboard types
- clear focus

## Definition of Done

All essential customer and admin actions remain usable without hover or precision pointer interaction.
