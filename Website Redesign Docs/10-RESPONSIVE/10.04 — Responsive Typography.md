# Responsive Typography

## Purpose

Maintain the TinyTots typographic hierarchy across viewport sizes.

## Principle

Typography should scale by hierarchy and reading context, not by arbitrary viewport percentages.

## Roles

Existing brand direction includes:

- Playfair Display for display/editorial use
- Geist/Inter direction for primary UI/body
- JetBrains Mono for SKU/technical identifiers where appropriate

Final font tokens remain governed by the design system.

## Display Type

Large editorial headlines may scale fluidly.

Use responsive clamps/tokens where appropriate rather than many breakpoint-specific font sizes.

## Body

Body text must preserve comfortable reading size and line height.

Do not shrink body text excessively to fit layouts.

## Navigation

Navigation typography should remain legible at all supported widths.

## Product Cards

Product names and prices require consistent hierarchy.

## Mobile Rules

Avoid:

- oversized headlines that consume the viewport
- extremely narrow text columns
- tiny metadata

## Accessibility

Text must maintain adequate contrast and usable sizing.

## Definition of Done

Typography retains the same brand hierarchy across desktop, tablet, and mobile without becoming cramped or oversized.
