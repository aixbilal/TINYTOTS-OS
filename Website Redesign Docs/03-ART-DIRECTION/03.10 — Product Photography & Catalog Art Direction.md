# TinyTots Product Photography & Catalog Art Direction

## 1. Purpose

Product presentation must balance fashion aspiration with ecommerce clarity.

## 2. Primary Product Image

The first image should clearly communicate:

- garment
- silhouette
- color
- overall styling

## 3. Secondary Images

Where available:

- back
- detail
- fabric
- styled look
- close-up
- scale/context

## 4. Background

Default product imagery should use a clean neutral background unless campaign context requires otherwise.

## 5. Consistency

Maintain consistent:

- aspect ratio
- crop
- lighting
- scale
- alignment

across product grids.

## 6. Product Grid

The grid should not be visually overloaded.

The image should dominate the card.

## 7. Hover

Preferred possibilities:

- second image
- subtle scale
- quick-action reveal

Only one or two should be used at a time.

## 8. Product Detail

Product detail should transition from emotional imagery to practical information.

Recommended hierarchy:

```text
IMAGE / GALLERY
↓
PRODUCT NAME
↓
PRICE
↓
COLOR
↓
SIZE
↓
ADD TO CART
↓
DETAILS
↓
DELIVERY / RETURNS
```

Actual ordering can vary after UX review.

## 9. Model Photography

Where models are used, photography should show the clothing clearly.

Do not let environmental storytelling obscure product information.

## 10. Acceptance Criteria

A customer should be able to appreciate the product aesthetically and evaluate it practically.
