# TinyTots Art Direction — Overview

## 1. Purpose

This phase translates the approved brand strategy into a concrete visual world.

It defines how TinyTots should look before individual pages are designed.

## 2. Art Direction Thesis

The visual world should combine:

> **quiet luxury + childhood warmth + contemporary fashion editorial**

The site should feel premium without becoming sterile and child-friendly without becoming childish.

## 3. Visual Priorities

Order of importance:

1. photography
2. composition
3. typography
4. whitespace
5. product presentation
6. color
7. texture/depth
8. motion
9. 3D/decorative effects

## 4. Visual Atmosphere

Preferred atmosphere:

- warm
- natural
- tactile
- calm
- refined
- cinematic
- understated
- human

## 5. Avoided Atmosphere

Avoid:

- overly colorful children's branding
- cartoon environments
- synthetic CGI as the dominant visual language
- neon futurism
- excessive gradients
- overly dark luxury
- excessive visual noise

## 6. Image Philosophy

Images should feel:

- authentic
- premium
- editorial
- emotionally believable
- naturally lit where possible
- fashion-conscious
- culturally appropriate

## 7. Composition Philosophy

Use a mixture of:

- full-bleed imagery
- controlled whitespace
- asymmetric layouts
- close-up details
- environmental portraits
- product-focused compositions

## 8. Background Philosophy

Preferred:

- warm ivory
- soft cream
- warm stone
- muted taupe
- charcoal/near-black for selected contrast moments

Backgrounds should support the image instead of competing with it.

## 9. Premium Principle

Do not attempt to make every viewport impressive.

Some sections should deliberately be quiet.

## 10. Art Direction Output

This phase establishes:

- visual mood
- color world
- photography direction
- image composition
- image treatment
- texture
- campaign language
- seasonal flexibility
- asset requirements
- reference-image rules

## 11. Acceptance Criteria

The art direction is complete when a designer can create a new TinyTots campaign image without inventing a new visual style each time.
