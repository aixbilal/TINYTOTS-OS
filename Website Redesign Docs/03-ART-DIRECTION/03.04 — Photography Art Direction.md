# TinyTots Photography Art Direction

## 1. Photography Is the Primary Brand Asset

Photography should carry more emotional weight than decorative UI.

## 2. Subject

Children should feel:

- natural
- confident
- expressive
- comfortable
- genuinely engaged

Avoid overly artificial smiles or rigid catalog posing in lifestyle imagery.

## 3. Age Range

The visual system should support multiple childhood stages appropriate to the actual TinyTots product range.

Do not invent ages for a campaign without checking the available products.

## 4. Styling

Preferred:

- sophisticated children's fashion
- layered neutral tones
- controlled color
- contemporary silhouettes
- authentic accessories

Avoid:

- costume-like styling
- excessive props
- distracting logos
- overly themed sets

## 5. Lighting

Preferred:

- soft natural light
- directional window light
- controlled studio light
- warm evening light where appropriate

Avoid:

- harsh flash as the default
- excessive artificial glow
- overprocessed skin

## 6. Environment

Potential environments:

- refined homes
- clean architectural spaces
- gardens
- streets with character
- natural landscapes
- quiet studio environments

## 7. Expression

Prioritize genuine:

- curiosity
- laughter
- concentration
- movement
- sibling connection
- quiet moments

## 8. Composition

Use:

- negative space
- unusual crops
- full-body compositions
- close-ups
- environmental portraits
- detail shots

## 9. Text Overlay

When an image supports text overlay, intentionally reserve negative space.

The image prompt/spec should define:

- text-safe region
- subject position
- visual weight
- crop tolerance

## 10. Aspect Ratios

The asset system should support:

- 16:9
- 21:9
- 4:5
- 3:4
- 1:1
- 9:16

The actual required ratios depend on page/component usage.

## 11. Product Photography

Product photography must prioritize:

- accurate color
- fabric detail
- silhouette
- consistent crop
- consistent lighting
- clean background

## 12. Lifestyle vs Product

Use both.

Lifestyle creates:

> desire and emotion

Product photography creates:

> confidence and understanding

## 13. AI-Generated Imagery

AI-generated imagery may be used for concept exploration or approved production assets only when:

- product accuracy is preserved
- anatomy is credible
- garments remain consistent
- image artifacts are absent
- usage rights are appropriate
- the image meets TinyTots quality standards

AI generation must not create fake product details.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

Photography should still look premium when all UI overlays are removed.
