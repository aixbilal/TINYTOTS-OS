# TinyTots Campaign & Seasonal Art Direction

## 1. Purpose

TinyTots needs a visual system that can evolve without losing identity.

## 2. Permanent Brand Layer

Must remain stable:

- typography
- foundational neutrals
- spacing
- component language
- interaction quality
- logo treatment
- core photography principles

## 3. Campaign Layer

May change:

- accent color
- campaign imagery
- seasonal styling
- background treatment
- editorial composition
- campaign-specific motion

## 4. Campaign Hierarchy

```text
TINY TOTS BRAND
       ↓
CAMPAIGN
       ↓
COLLECTION
       ↓
PRODUCT
```

Campaign must never overpower the master brand.

## 5. Seasonal Examples

Possible directions:

### Spring

- soft greens
- warm daylight
- botanical environments

### Summer

- brighter natural light
- outdoor environments
- airy compositions

### Autumn

- warm earth tones
- textured environments
- deeper contrast

### Winter

- cream
- charcoal
- muted berry
- warm indoor light

These are conceptual directions, not fixed palettes.

## 6. Campaign Hero

Campaign heroes may use more dramatic composition than ordinary product pages.

## 7. Campaign Motion

Allowed:

- cinematic reveal
- image transition
- controlled parallax
- large typography movement

## 8. Campaign 3D

3D can be used for a major campaign when it has a clear concept.

It should not be used merely because the technology is available.

## 9. CMS Compatibility

Campaign art direction must work with the existing campaign/signage content model where applicable.

Any new CMS fields must be documented separately.

## 10. Acceptance Criteria

A new campaign can look fresh while still being immediately recognizable as TinyTots.
