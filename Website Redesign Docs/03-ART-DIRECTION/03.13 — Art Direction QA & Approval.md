# TinyTots Art Direction QA & Approval

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how the art direction is evaluated before it becomes implementation guidance.

## 2. Brand Test

Does the visual direction communicate:

- premium
- children's fashion
- warmth
- confidence
- modernity

## 3. Editorial Test

Can the same direction produce:

- homepage hero
- collection story
- product page
- blog/editorial
- campaign

without looking inconsistent?

## 4. Product Test

Place a real product image into the proposed environment.

Check:

- readability
- color interaction
- hierarchy
- crop
- visual distraction

## 5. Typography Test

Test:

- hero
- navigation
- product
- long text
- mobile

## 6. Color Test

Test:

- text contrast
- CTA
- product images
- dark sections
- admin UI
- campaign accents

## 7. Motion Test

Before adding motion, verify that static composition works.

## 8. 3D Test

Before approving 3D, answer:

1. Why is 3D better?
2. What is the fallback?
3. What is the mobile behavior?
4. What is the performance cost?
5. What happens with reduced motion?

## 9. Reference Test

For every major reference:

- identify principle
- identify adaptation
- identify difference from source

## 10. Rejection Conditions

Reject the direction if:

- it feels childish
- it looks like a generic luxury template
- effects overpower products
- color reduces readability
- imagery feels artificial
- performance would obviously suffer
- the direction cannot scale across pages

## 11. Approval Record

Approval should record:

```text
DATE
DECISION
APPROVED DIRECTION
OPEN QUESTIONS
AFFECTED DOCUMENTS
NEXT PHASE
```

## 12. Definition of Done

Phase 03 is complete when:

- mood direction is approved
- color direction is approved
- photography direction is approved
- composition rules are approved
- campaign flexibility is defined
- asset rules are defined
- reference-library rules are established
- QA criteria are documented
