# TinyTots Editorial Art Direction

## 1. Purpose

Editorial content should make TinyTots feel like a fashion brand rather than a product catalog.

## 2. Editorial Content Types

Potential:

- collection stories
- seasonal campaigns
- Our Story
- style guides
- journal/blog
- behind-the-scenes
- material stories

## 3. Composition

Editorial pages may use:

- large typography
- asymmetry
- full-bleed photography
- mixed image sizes
- overlapping sections
- generous whitespace

## 4. Text

Editorial copy should be:

- concise where possible
- meaningful
- easy to scan
- visually integrated

## 5. Typography

Use display typography for emotional statements.

Use UI/body typography for information.

## 6. Image Sequence

A strong editorial page may use:

```text
HERO
↓
QUIET TEXT
↓
FULL IMAGE
↓
DETAIL
↓
STORY
↓
PRODUCT
↓
CTA
```

The sequence should vary by content.

## 7. Interaction

Editorial interactions can be more expressive than utility pages.

## 8. Avoid

Do not make every editorial page a slideshow.

Do not make every section animate on scroll.

## 9. Acceptance Criteria

Editorial content should feel visually distinct from standard CRUD/product-list layouts while remaining within the same brand system.
