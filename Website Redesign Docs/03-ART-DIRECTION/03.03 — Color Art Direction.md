# TinyTots Color Art Direction

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the visual logic behind the color palette.

Exact production tokens belong to the design-system implementation after visual validation.

## 2. Primary World

The foundation should live in warm neutrals.

Conceptual spectrum:

```text
WARM IVORY
     ↓
CREAM
     ↓
SAND
     ↓
WARM STONE
     ↓
TAUPE
     ↓
CHARCOAL
     ↓
NEAR BLACK
```

## 3. Primary Surface

The main light surface should feel warmer than pure white.

Avoid clinical white as the dominant background unless required for a specific product image.

## 4. Primary Text

Primary text should be a deep neutral rather than absolute black in most light contexts.

This creates softer contrast while retaining readability.

## 5. Accent

A restrained warm accent may be retained from the existing TinyTots direction.

Potential territory:

- muted terracotta
- dusty coral
- warm rose
- subdued clay

The exact hue must be selected through visual testing.

> **DECIDED (2026-08-16):** Visual testing concluded with **olive green**, not
> terracotta. Approved hex: `#616845`, sampled directly from the approved
> final homepage reference (`THE_HOMEPAGe_FINAL.png`), verified at 5.87:1
> contrast against white text (WCAG AA pass for large/button text). This
> supersedes an earlier implementation pass that used `#8f5030` (terracotta)
> before the final reference was available — that value has been replaced
> project-wide (`--color-brand-primary` in `app/globals.css` and all
> hardcoded references) to `#616845`. Terracotta remains documented above
> as the original exploratory direction for historical context, but is no
> longer the accent in use.

## 6. Accent Usage

Accent should appear:

- intentionally
- sparingly
- consistently

Good uses:

- selected states
- important CTA
- campaign detail
- small highlights

Bad uses:

- every button
- every link
- every border
- every icon

## 7. Dark Palette

Use charcoal/near-black for selected moments:

- footer
- editorial transition
- campaign
- premium contrast
- special product presentation

## 8. Seasonal Flexibility

The foundation must support campaign-specific accents without breaking the brand.

A seasonal campaign may introduce:

- sage
- dusty blue
- muted berry
- soft ochre

but these should be campaign tokens, not permanent foundation colors.

## 9. Color Proportion

A useful starting principle:

```text
NEUTRAL SURFACES     █████████████████
DARK TEXT/CONTRAST   ███
BRAND ACCENT         █
CAMPAIGN COLOR       optional
```

## 10. Photography Interaction

The UI palette should not compete with colorful product photography.

When product imagery is visually rich, surrounding UI should become quieter.

## 11. Dark-on-Light Principle

The primary experience should remain light and warm.

A fully dark luxury storefront is not the default direction.

## 12. Finalization Requirements

Before locking HEX values:

- compare against brand imagery
- test text contrast
- test product photography
- test buttons
- test navigation
- test mobile
- test admin
