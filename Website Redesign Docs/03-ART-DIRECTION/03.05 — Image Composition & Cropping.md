# TinyTots Image Composition & Cropping

## 1. Purpose

The same photograph can feel premium or ordinary depending on crop and placement.

## 2. Composition Modes

### Hero

Large image with strong visual subject and text-safe area.

### Editorial

Image may intentionally break the standard grid.

### Product

Controlled ratio and consistent object placement.

### Detail

Close crop emphasizing:

- fabric
- stitching
- texture
- accessory
- silhouette

### Lifestyle

Environment remains visible enough to communicate context.

## 3. Focal Point

Every major image must have an intentional focal point.

## 4. Focal Point Metadata

Where CMS support exists, campaign assets should support:

- crop
- focal point
- desktop crop
- mobile crop

## 5. Mobile Crop

Never assume the desktop crop can simply be centered on mobile.

Critical subjects must remain visible.

## 6. Text Safe Area

Images with text overlay should reserve sufficient low-detail space.

## 7. Cropping Rules

Avoid cutting:

- faces
- hands
- product-critical garment details

unless the crop is intentionally editorial.

## 8. Product Grid Consistency

Product cards should use consistent image ratios even when source images differ.

## 9. Full Bleed

Full-bleed images should reach the viewport edge without accidental padding.

## 10. Overlap

Image overlap is permitted for editorial storytelling.

It must still maintain:

- responsive integrity
- readable text
- performance
- accessibility

## 11. Acceptance Criteria

Every major image component must define its crop behavior at each responsive class.
