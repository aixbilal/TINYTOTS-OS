# TinyTots Background & Environment System

## 1. Background Roles

Define:

- `canvas-light`
- `canvas-warm`
- `canvas-dark`
- `section-contrast`
- `image-fullbleed`
- `overlay`

## 2. Light Backgrounds

Primary background territory:

- warm ivory
- soft cream
- subtle stone

## 3. Dark Backgrounds

Use strategically for:

- footer
- campaign transition
- premium editorial section
- special content

## 4. Gradient Use

Gradients are allowed only when they support:

- image readability
- depth
- atmospheric transition

Avoid decorative gradient backgrounds as the default.

## 5. Environmental Photography

Environmental sections should create a sense of place.

Potential visual themes:

- refined interiors
- natural outdoor spaces
- architectural details
- quiet family environments

## 6. Background Motion

Potential:

- slow image drift
- masked reveal
- subtle parallax

Avoid constant animated backgrounds.

## 7. 3D Environments

3D environments may be explored for campaign-specific experiences.

They require:

- clear purpose
- static fallback
- performance plan
- mobile strategy

## 8. Acceptance Criteria

A background must improve content hierarchy or atmosphere.
