# Team Management

## Purpose

Manage internal team records only where this is an actual supported business feature.

## Potential Fields

- name
- role
- image
- status
- display order
- public visibility

## Privacy

Do not expose internal-only information to the public frontend.

## Permissions

Team record management must be separated from authentication/authorization management unless intentionally linked by the backend.

## Definition of Done

Team content can be maintained safely without confusing public profiles with admin identities.
