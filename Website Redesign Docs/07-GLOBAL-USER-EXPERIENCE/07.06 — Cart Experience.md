# TinyTots Global UX — Cart Experience

## Purpose

The cart should feel calm, trustworthy, and frictionless.

## Cart Entry

Cart should be accessible globally.

A cart count may communicate item quantity without becoming visually dominant.

## Cart Drawer

A drawer can provide rapid review while preserving shopping context.

It should include:

- product
- variant
- quantity
- price
- remove action
- subtotal
- checkout CTA

## Full Cart

A dedicated cart route may provide more space for:

- detailed review
- promotional messaging
- delivery information
- quantity management

## Quantity

Quantity controls must:

- prevent invalid values
- communicate updates
- preserve product variant

## Updates

Cart changes should feel immediate.

Avoid unnecessary page reloads.

## Empty Cart

Use a premium but useful state:

```text
Your cart is empty.

[Continue Shopping]
```

Optional curated discovery may be added without overwhelming the user.

## Motion

Allowed:

- drawer entrance
- item removal transition
- quantity feedback
- subtotal update

Avoid:

- blocking animations before checkout

## Trust

Important information should remain visible where relevant:

- shipping
- returns
- secure checkout messaging

Do not create unsupported claims.

## Definition of Done

A user can review and modify the cart quickly and confidently.
