# TinyTots Global UX — Product Discovery

## Purpose

Product discovery connects brand storytelling with efficient shopping.

## Discovery Layers

```text
HOMEPAGE
↓
COLLECTION
↓
CATEGORY
↓
PRODUCT
```

Search and direct links may enter at any level.

## Product Card

A product card should communicate:

- image
- name
- price
- relevant variant information
- availability state where needed

## Hover

Desktop may reveal:

- secondary image
- quick action
- subtle motion

Do not hide essential product information behind hover.

## Quick Add

If implemented, it must not replace the product detail page where details matter.

## Editorial Discovery

Curated collections may combine:

- campaign image
- product selection
- editorial copy

The relationship between editorial and commerce should be intentional.

## Pagination / Infinite Loading

Choose based on actual catalog behavior and performance.

Do not use infinite scroll merely because it looks modern.

## Product Ranking

Ranking logic must be separated from presentation.

The UI must not imply editorial ranking if the backend uses a different ordering model.

## Empty Results

Provide:

- useful explanation
- category links
- search alternative
- reset filters

## Definition of Done

Users can browse products efficiently without losing the premium editorial experience.
