# Photography Moodboard

## Purpose

Define the photographic language that supports TinyTots' premium positioning.

## Direction

Photography should feel:

- candid
- natural
- editorial
- warm
- tactile
- premium
- emotionally believable

## Subject Direction

Children should appear naturally engaged rather than overly posed.

Potential settings:

- refined interiors
- natural outdoor environments
- quiet family moments
- premium editorial spaces

## Styling

Product presentation should remain the hero.

Avoid backgrounds that compete with garments.

## Lighting

Prefer:

- natural light
- soft directional light
- subtle shadow
- realistic skin tones
- controlled contrast

Avoid obviously synthetic lighting unless deliberately used for a campaign concept.

## Composition

Useful compositions include:

- negative space for typography
- full-body editorial frames
- close garment details
- environmental portraits
- asymmetrical framing

## Image Ratios

Reference library should include examples for:

- 16:9
- wide hero
- product portrait
- square
- mobile portrait

Final production ratios remain page-specific.

## Image Treatment

Avoid heavy filters.

Color grading should preserve:

- fabric accuracy
- natural skin
- neutral surroundings

## Reference Selection

Every reference should identify:

- composition
- lighting
- styling
- crop
- intended use

## Definition of Done

Photography makes TinyTots look like a premium children's fashion brand rather than a generic children's retailer.
