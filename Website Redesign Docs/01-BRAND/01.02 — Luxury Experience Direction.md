# TinyTots Luxury Experience Direction

## 1. Experience Thesis

The redesign should feel like entering a carefully curated fashion environment rather than opening a conventional ecommerce application.

The experience should progress through:

```text
DISCOVER
   ↓
DESIRE
   ↓
UNDERSTAND
   ↓
TRUST
   ↓
ACT
```

## 2. Visual Rhythm

The website should alternate between:

- cinematic moments
- quiet whitespace
- product-focused moments
- editorial storytelling
- functional utility

Do not make every section visually loud.

## 3. Hero Philosophy

The hero should establish the brand immediately.

Preferred characteristics:

- strong photography or carefully controlled motion
- minimal copy
- clear hierarchy
- one dominant action where appropriate
- generous negative space
- deliberate composition

Avoid:

- multiple competing CTAs
- excessive floating badges
- excessive decorative particles
- generic gradient hero backgrounds

## 4. Product Philosophy

Products are the hero of an ecommerce brand.

Product presentation should prioritize:

- accurate imagery
- useful information
- clean browsing
- consistent image ratios
- controlled hover behavior
- clear size/color availability
- easy transition into product details

## 5. Editorial Philosophy

Editorial sections may break the normal grid.

Approved techniques include:

- asymmetric image/text arrangements
- large typography
- edge-to-edge photography
- layered compositions
- controlled cropping
- editorial whitespace

## 6. Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel:

- responsive
- tactile
- calm
- deliberate

A button should not need a dramatic animation to feel premium.

## 7. Premium Depth

Depth may be created through:

- typography scale
- whitespace
- layering
- image overlap
- subtle shadows
- motion
- controlled 3D

Do not default to:

- glassmorphism
- glowing borders
- heavy blur
- floating cards everywhere

## 8. Navigation

Navigation should prioritize discovery without becoming a visual obstacle.

The final system should investigate:

- minimal primary navigation
- intelligent mega menu
- search-first discovery
- contextual transitions
- mobile full-screen navigation

## 9. Scroll Experience

Scrolling should reveal the story progressively.

Potential techniques:

- image reveals
- typography reveals
- sticky editorial sections
- subtle parallax
- section transitions

Avoid constant scroll-jacking.

## 10. Luxury Through Restraint

A page with:

- one excellent image
- one excellent headline
- one clear action
- excellent typography
- excellent spacing

may feel more premium than a page containing 15 animations.

## 11. Admin Translation

The Admin experience should use the same:

- palette
- typography
- spacing principles
- interaction quality

but with:

- higher density
- faster transitions
- stronger data hierarchy
- less decorative motion

## 12. Acceptance Criteria

A user should feel:

> "This brand cares about details."

An admin user should feel:

> "This system is under control."
