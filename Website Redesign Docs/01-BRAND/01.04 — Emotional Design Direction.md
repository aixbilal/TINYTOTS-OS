# TinyTots Emotional Design Direction

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the emotional journey the interface should create.

## 2. Primary Emotional Arc

### Stage 1 — Curiosity

The first viewport should create a reason to continue.

Tools:

- strong image
- intriguing composition
- restrained headline
- subtle motion

### Stage 2 — Warmth

The visitor should feel that the brand understands childhood and family life.

Tools:

- authentic lifestyle photography
- natural expressions
- warm tonal treatment
- human storytelling

### Stage 3 — Desire

Product presentation should create visual desire without pressure.

Tools:

- large product imagery
- clean backgrounds
- detail shots
- editorial product compositions

### Stage 4 — Confidence

The interface must make purchase decisions easier.

Tools:

- clear information
- size guidance
- availability
- shipping/returns clarity
- reviews/testimonials where available

### Stage 5 — Delight

Small moments can reward interaction.

Tools:

- subtle hover behavior
- tactile controls
- elegant transitions
- carefully chosen microinteractions

## 3. Emotional Design Rule

Do not use anxiety-based design patterns.

Avoid:

- unnecessary countdowns
- aggressive urgency
- fake scarcity
- excessive popups
- manipulative interruption

unless a real business rule requires the information.

## 4. Emotional Role of Motion

Motion should amplify an emotion that already exists in the content.

Examples:

| Content | Appropriate motion |
|---|---|
| Hero | slow reveal |
| Product | tactile hover |
| Editorial | image/text reveal |
| Search | smooth expansion |
| Cart | spatial transition |
| Success | subtle confirmation |

## 5. Emotional Role of Color

Color should establish mood before it communicates interaction.

Semantic roles must be defined later in the design system.

## 6. Emotional Role of Sound

Sound is not a default experience.

If explored later, it must:

- be opt-in
- never interfere with accessibility
- never autoplay unexpectedly
- have an obvious control

## 7. Mobile Emotional Experience

Mobile must preserve:

- brand calmness
- image impact
- product confidence
- touch quality

Do not sacrifice hierarchy simply to fit more content.

## 8. Acceptance Criteria

The experience should create desire and confidence without relying on pressure or spectacle.
